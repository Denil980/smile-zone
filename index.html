MEET OUR SPECIALIST

About Dr. Lajja Kamdar

Dr. Lajja Kamdar is a well-reputed Dentist in Tilak Nagar, Chembur. She acquired her degree of bachelor's in 'Dental Surgery' from the prestigious Nair Hospital Dental College, Mumbai in the year 2000. Dr. Lajja has always kept her skills updated with the latest certified courses related to Cosmetic Dentistry, Restorative Dentistry, and Pediatric Dentistry. She's specialized in Full Mouth Rehabilitation with her expertise in SMILE DESIGN involving the latest in dental technology which includes state-of-the-art Flexible Dentures and Dental Implants.
